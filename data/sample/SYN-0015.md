# Phase 1/2 Study of Vaxaria, an Investigational Vaccine Against Seasonal Respiratory Virus X

## Summary
A fictional Phase 1/2, randomized, observer-blind, placebo-controlled study of
Vaxaria, an investigational mRNA vaccine against the fictional seasonal
respiratory virus X, in healthy adults over 12 months.

## Condition
Prevention of symptomatic infection with the fictional seasonal respiratory
virus X in healthy adults.

## Intervention
Two doses of Vaxaria 50 mcg given intramuscularly 28 days apart versus saline
placebo, randomized 1:1.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: healthy adults 18 to 59 years; no prior documented infection with
virus X. Exclusion: immunocompromising condition; receipt of any investigational
vaccine within 3 months; history of severe allergic reaction to a vaccine
component; pregnancy.

## Primary Endpoints
Safety and reactogenicity through 28 days after the second dose, and geometric
mean neutralizing antibody titers at day 56.

## Secondary Endpoints
Seroconversion rate at day 56; incidence of laboratory-confirmed symptomatic
virus X infection through month 12; durability of antibody response.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, Vaxaria induced an 18-fold rise in neutralizing
antibody titers at day 56 with mostly mild, transient reactogenicity.
