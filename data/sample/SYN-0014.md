# Phase 2 Study of Coliva in Moderate Ulcerative Colitis

## Summary
A fictional Phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled induction
study of Coliva, an investigational oral agent, in adults with moderately active
ulcerative colitis over an 8-week induction period.

## Condition
Moderately active ulcerative colitis with a modified Mayo score of 5 to 9 and an
endoscopic subscore of at least 2.

## Intervention
Coliva 30 mg orally once daily versus placebo, randomized 2:1.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: adults 18 to 70 years; ulcerative colitis diagnosed for at least 3
months; inadequate response to at least one conventional therapy. Exclusion:
Crohn's disease or indeterminate colitis; prior colectomy; active Clostridioides
difficile infection; use of biologics within 8 weeks.

## Primary Endpoints
Proportion achieving clinical remission (modified Mayo score of 2 or less with
no subscore above 1) at week 8.

## Secondary Endpoints
Proportion achieving endoscopic improvement; clinical response; change in fecal
calprotectin.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, 33% of the Coliva arm achieved clinical remission at
week 8 versus 13% on placebo.
