# Phase 3 Trial of Osteobind for Postmenopausal Osteoporosis

## Summary
A fictional Phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of
Osteobind, an investigational twice-yearly injectable, in postmenopausal women
with osteoporosis over 24 months.

## Condition
Postmenopausal osteoporosis with a bone mineral density T-score of -2.5 or lower
at the lumbar spine or total hip.

## Intervention
Osteobind 60 mg subcutaneous every 6 months versus placebo, both with daily
calcium and vitamin D, randomized 1:1.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: postmenopausal women 55 to 85 years; T-score of -2.5 or lower.
Exclusion: prior hip fracture; current bisphosphonate use; serum calcium outside
the normal range; vitamin D deficiency below 20 ng/mL at screening.

## Primary Endpoints
Incidence of new vertebral fractures through month 24.

## Secondary Endpoints
Incidence of nonvertebral fractures; percent change in lumbar-spine and total-hip
bone mineral density; change in bone-turnover markers.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, Osteobind reduced new vertebral fractures by a
relative 68% and increased lumbar-spine bone mineral density by 9.1%.
