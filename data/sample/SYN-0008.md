# Phase 2 Study of Hepaclear in Chronic Hepatitis Caused by Fatty Liver

## Summary
A fictional Phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of
Hepaclear, an investigational oral agent, in adults with biopsy-confirmed
nonalcoholic steatohepatitis over 48 weeks.

## Condition
Nonalcoholic steatohepatitis (NASH) with liver fibrosis stage F2 to F3 confirmed
by biopsy.

## Intervention
Hepaclear 25 mg orally once daily versus placebo, randomized 1:1.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: adults 18 to 75 years; biopsy-confirmed NASH with a nonalcoholic
fatty liver disease activity score of at least 4. Exclusion: significant alcohol
use; other chronic liver disease; decompensated cirrhosis; type 1 diabetes.

## Primary Endpoints
Proportion achieving resolution of steatohepatitis without worsening of fibrosis
at week 48, assessed by liver biopsy.

## Secondary Endpoints
Proportion with at least one-stage fibrosis improvement; change in liver fat by
MRI proton density fat fraction; change in alanine aminotransferase.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, 31% of the Hepaclear arm achieved steatohepatitis
resolution versus 12% on placebo.

## Status
Active, not recruiting (synthetic record).
