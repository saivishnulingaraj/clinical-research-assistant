# Phase 3 Trial of Immunova as Adjuvant Therapy in Resected Melanoma

## Summary
A fictional Phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of
Immunova, an investigational immune checkpoint inhibitor, as adjuvant therapy in
adults with completely resected high-risk melanoma over a 12-month treatment
period.

## Condition
Completely resected stage IIB to IIIC cutaneous melanoma at high risk of
recurrence.

## Intervention
Immunova 400 mg intravenous every 6 weeks versus placebo for up to 12 months,
randomized 1:1.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: adults 18 years and older; completely resected high-risk melanoma
within 12 weeks; ECOG performance status 0 or 1. Exclusion: mucosal or uveal
melanoma; active autoimmune disease requiring systemic treatment; prior
checkpoint inhibitor therapy.

## Primary Endpoints
Recurrence-free survival, defined as time from randomization to first recurrence
or death from any cause.

## Secondary Endpoints
Distant metastasis-free survival; overall survival; safety and incidence of
immune-related adverse events.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, Immunova improved 2-year recurrence-free survival to
72% versus 58% with placebo.

## Status
Active, not recruiting (synthetic record).
