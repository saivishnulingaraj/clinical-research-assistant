# Phase 2 Study of Pulmoflow in Moderate-to-Severe Asthma

## Summary
A fictional Phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of
Pulmoflow, an investigational inhaled biologic, in adults with moderate-to-severe
eosinophilic asthma over 24 weeks.

## Condition
Moderate-to-severe asthma with a blood eosinophil count of at least 300 cells
per microliter and at least two exacerbations in the prior year.

## Intervention
Pulmoflow 100 mcg inhaled twice daily versus matching placebo, on top of
standard inhaled corticosteroid plus long-acting beta-agonist therapy.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: adults 18 to 70 years; physician-diagnosed asthma for at least 1
year; pre-bronchodilator FEV1 between 40% and 80% predicted. Exclusion: current
smoker or smoking history above 10 pack-years; recent respiratory infection;
other chronic lung disease.

## Primary Endpoints
Annualized rate of moderate-to-severe asthma exacerbations over 24 weeks.

## Secondary Endpoints
Change in pre-bronchodilator FEV1; change in Asthma Control Questionnaire score;
change in blood eosinophil count.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, Pulmoflow reduced the annualized exacerbation rate by
54% versus placebo and improved FEV1 by 130 mL.
