# Phase 2 Study of Neurozen in Early Alzheimer's Disease

## Summary
A fictional Phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of
Neurozen, an investigational monoclonal antibody, in adults with early
symptomatic Alzheimer's disease over 18 months.

## Condition
Early symptomatic Alzheimer's disease (mild cognitive impairment or mild
dementia) with confirmed amyloid pathology.

## Intervention
Neurozen 10 mg/kg intravenous infusion every 4 weeks versus placebo infusion,
randomized 1:1.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: adults 50 to 85 years; Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) score of
22 to 30; positive amyloid PET or cerebrospinal-fluid biomarker. Exclusion:
more than four cerebral microhemorrhages on MRI; anticoagulant use; other
causes of dementia.

## Primary Endpoints
Change from baseline in the Clinical Dementia Rating Sum of Boxes (CDR-SB) at
month 18.

## Secondary Endpoints
Change in amyloid PET standardized uptake value ratio; change in MMSE; incidence
of amyloid-related imaging abnormalities (ARIA).

## Results
In this fictional dataset, Neurozen slowed CDR-SB decline by 27% versus placebo;
ARIA-edema occurred in 9% of treated participants, mostly asymptomatic.
