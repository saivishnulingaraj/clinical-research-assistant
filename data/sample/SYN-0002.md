# Phase 3 Trial of Cardizil versus Standard Care in Chronic Heart Failure

## Summary
A fictional Phase 3, multicenter, open-label trial comparing Cardizil, an
investigational once-daily injectable, with standard guideline-directed medical
therapy in adults with chronic heart failure with reduced ejection fraction.

## Condition
Chronic heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF), New York Heart
Association (NYHA) class II to III, with left ventricular ejection fraction of
40% or less.

## Intervention
Cardizil 1 mg subcutaneous once daily added to background therapy, versus
standard guideline-directed medical therapy alone.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: adults 40 years and older; documented HFrEF for at least 3 months;
stable background therapy for 4 weeks. Exclusion: recent myocardial infarction
within 90 days; systolic blood pressure below 90 mmHg; serum potassium above
5.5 mmol/L; eGFR below 30 mL/min.

## Primary Endpoints
Time to first occurrence of cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart
failure over a median follow-up of 24 months.

## Secondary Endpoints
Change in NYHA class; change in NT-proBNP; total heart-failure hospitalizations;
change in 6-minute walk distance.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, Cardizil reduced the primary composite endpoint by a
relative 22% versus standard care (hazard ratio 0.78).

## Status
Active, not recruiting (synthetic record).
