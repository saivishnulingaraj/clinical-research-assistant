# A Phase 2 Study of Velpramine for Moderate Plaque Psoriasis

## Summary
This fictional Phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study
evaluates Velpramine, an investigational oral small molecule, in adults with
moderate plaque psoriasis over a 16-week treatment period.

## Condition
Moderate plaque psoriasis, defined as a baseline Psoriasis Area and Severity
Index (PASI) score between 10 and 20 and body surface area involvement of 5% to 10%.

## Intervention
Velpramine 20 mg taken orally once daily, compared against a matching placebo.
Randomization was 2:1 (active:placebo).

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: adults aged 18 to 65 years; diagnosis of plaque psoriasis for at
least 12 months; PASI between 10 and 20. Exclusion: prior exposure to systemic
biologics within 6 months; active infection; pregnancy or breastfeeding;
estimated glomerular filtration rate below 45 mL/min.

## Primary Endpoints
Proportion of participants achieving a 75% reduction in PASI (PASI 75) at week 16.

## Secondary Endpoints
PASI 90 at week 16; change in Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI) from baseline;
proportion achieving a static Physician Global Assessment score of 0 or 1.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, 48% of the Velpramine arm achieved PASI 75 at week 16
versus 11% on placebo. DLQI improved by a mean of 6.2 points on active treatment.
