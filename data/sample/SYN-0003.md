# Phase 1 Dose-Escalation Study of Oncoral in Advanced Solid Tumors

## Summary
A fictional Phase 1, open-label, dose-escalation study assessing the safety and
tolerability of Oncoral, an investigational oral kinase inhibitor, in adults
with advanced or metastatic solid tumors refractory to standard therapy.

## Condition
Advanced or metastatic solid tumors that have progressed after at least one
prior line of systemic therapy.

## Intervention
Oncoral administered orally once daily using a 3+3 dose-escalation design across
dose levels of 50, 100, 200, and 400 mg.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: adults 18 years and older; Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG)
performance status 0 or 1; measurable disease per RECIST 1.1; adequate organ
function. Exclusion: untreated brain metastases; QTc interval above 470 ms;
major surgery within 28 days.

## Primary Endpoints
Incidence of dose-limiting toxicities during the first treatment cycle and
determination of the maximum tolerated dose.

## Secondary Endpoints
Objective response rate per RECIST 1.1; pharmacokinetic parameters including
maximum plasma concentration and area under the curve.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, the maximum tolerated dose was 200 mg once daily;
the most common dose-limiting toxicity was reversible grade 3 transaminitis.
