# Phase 3 Trial of Rheumavex in Active Rheumatoid Arthritis

## Summary
A fictional Phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of
Rheumavex, an investigational oral JAK inhibitor, in adults with moderately to
severely active rheumatoid arthritis with an inadequate response to methotrexate.

## Condition
Moderately to severely active rheumatoid arthritis despite stable methotrexate,
with at least six swollen and six tender joints.

## Intervention
Rheumavex 15 mg orally once daily versus placebo, both added to background
methotrexate, randomized 2:1.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: adults 18 years and older; rheumatoid arthritis for at least 6
months; stable methotrexate for 8 weeks. Exclusion: active or latent
tuberculosis; absolute neutrophil count below 1000 per microliter; serious
infection within 3 months; prior JAK inhibitor exposure.

## Primary Endpoints
Proportion of participants achieving a 20% improvement in American College of
Rheumatology criteria (ACR20) at week 12.

## Secondary Endpoints
ACR50 and ACR70 at week 12; change in Disease Activity Score 28 (DAS28);
change in Health Assessment Questionnaire Disability Index.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, 63% of the Rheumavex arm achieved ACR20 at week 12
versus 29% on placebo.
