# Phase 2 Study of Renopro in Diabetic Kidney Disease

## Summary
A fictional Phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of
Renopro, an investigational oral agent, in adults with diabetic kidney disease
over 26 weeks.

## Condition
Diabetic kidney disease in adults with type 2 diabetes, an eGFR of 25 to 75
mL/min, and a urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio of 200 to 5000 mg/g.

## Intervention
Renopro 10 mg orally once daily versus placebo, both on a stable renin-angiotensin
system inhibitor, randomized 1:1.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: adults 18 to 75 years; type 2 diabetes; stable ACE inhibitor or ARB
for 4 weeks. Exclusion: nondiabetic kidney disease; serum potassium above 5.0
mmol/L; recent acute kidney injury; dialysis.

## Primary Endpoints
Percent change from baseline in the urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio at
week 26.

## Secondary Endpoints
Change in eGFR; proportion with at least a 30% reduction in albuminuria; change
in systolic blood pressure.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, Renopro reduced the urinary albumin-to-creatinine
ratio by 38% versus 6% on placebo.

## Status
Active, not recruiting (synthetic record).
