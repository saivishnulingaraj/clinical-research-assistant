# Phase 3 Trial of Migratol for Prevention of Chronic Migraine

## Summary
A fictional Phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of
Migratol, an investigational monthly injectable, for the preventive treatment of
chronic migraine in adults over 12 weeks.

## Condition
Chronic migraine, defined as at least 15 headache days per month with at least 8
migraine days per month for more than 3 months.

## Intervention
Migratol 120 mg subcutaneous once monthly versus placebo, randomized 1:1.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: adults 18 to 65 years; chronic migraine for at least 12 months; onset
before age 50. Exclusion: medication-overuse headache from opioids; prior use of
a CGRP-targeted therapy; uncontrolled hypertension; pregnancy.

## Primary Endpoints
Change from baseline in the mean number of monthly migraine days over weeks 9
through 12.

## Secondary Endpoints
Proportion achieving at least a 50% reduction in monthly migraine days; change in
acute medication use days; change in migraine-related disability score.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, Migratol reduced monthly migraine days by 4.9 versus
2.1 on placebo.
