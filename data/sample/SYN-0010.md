# Phase 2 Study of Dermaclear in Moderate Atopic Dermatitis

## Summary
A fictional Phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of
Dermaclear, an investigational topical cream, in adults with moderate atopic
dermatitis over 12 weeks.

## Condition
Moderate atopic dermatitis with an Investigator's Global Assessment (IGA) score
of 3 and an Eczema Area and Severity Index (EASI) of 12 to 21.

## Intervention
Dermaclear 1% cream applied twice daily versus vehicle cream, randomized 2:1.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: adults 18 to 65 years; atopic dermatitis for at least 1 year;
affected body surface area of 5% to 20%. Exclusion: use of systemic
immunosuppressants within 4 weeks; active skin infection; concurrent use of
other topical treatments on affected areas.

## Primary Endpoints
Proportion achieving an IGA score of 0 or 1 with at least a 2-grade improvement
at week 12.

## Secondary Endpoints
Proportion achieving EASI 75; change in weekly average Peak Pruritus Numerical
Rating Scale; change in affected body surface area.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, 41% of the Dermaclear arm achieved the primary IGA
response versus 15% on vehicle.
