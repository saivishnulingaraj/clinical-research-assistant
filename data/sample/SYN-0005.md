# Phase 3 Trial of Glucoran in Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

## Summary
A fictional Phase 3, randomized, double-blind, active-controlled trial of
Glucoran, an investigational once-weekly injectable, versus an active comparator
in adults with inadequately controlled type 2 diabetes.

## Condition
Type 2 diabetes mellitus inadequately controlled on metformin monotherapy, with
baseline glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c) between 7.5% and 10.5%.

## Intervention
Glucoran 2 mg subcutaneous once weekly versus the active comparator once weekly,
both added to stable metformin, randomized 1:1.

## Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: adults 18 to 75 years; type 2 diabetes for at least 6 months; stable
metformin at least 1500 mg daily. Exclusion: type 1 diabetes; history of
pancreatitis; personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma; eGFR
below 30 mL/min.

## Primary Endpoints
Change from baseline in HbA1c at week 40.

## Secondary Endpoints
Proportion achieving HbA1c below 7.0%; change in body weight; change in fasting
plasma glucose; proportion with documented hypoglycemia.

## Results
In this fictional dataset, Glucoran reduced HbA1c by 1.4 percentage points
versus 1.0 for the comparator, with a mean weight loss of 3.8 kg.

## Status
Active, not recruiting (synthetic record).
